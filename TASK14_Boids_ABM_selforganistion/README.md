__Boids__ is an artificial life algorithm, developed by Craig Reynolds in 1986, 
which simulates the flocking behaviour of birds, and related group motion. 
His paper on this topic was published in 1987 in the proceedings of the 
ACM SIGGRAPH conference.
The name "boid" corresponds to a shortened version of "bird-oid object", which 
refers to a bird-like object, as well as referencing the stereotypical New York 
pronunciation of 'bird' as /ˈbəɪd/.
Reynolds' boid model is one example of a larger general concept, for which many 
other variations have been developed since. The closely related work of Ichiro 
Aoki is noteworthy because it was published in 1982 – five years before 
Reynolds' boids paper.
